The mapping
M = log2( β / (1 − β) )

Local stretch dM/dβ = 1 / (ln 2 · β(1 − β)): about 5.8 at β = 0.5 and about 38 at β = 0.04 or 0.96.

fM(m) = fβ(β) · ln 2 · β(1 − β)

Change of variables: probability mass is conserved, so the density in view A is divided by the local stretch as the axis opens.

Noise model (view C): methylated and unmethylated intensities each get independent log-normal noise, so M = log2(Meth / Unmeth) carries additive noise of SD σ√2 / ln 2. This is a simulation assumption.
